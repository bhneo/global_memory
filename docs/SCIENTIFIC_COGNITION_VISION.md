# Scientific Cognition Vision

Galois aims to be a local, auditable, self-evolving scientific cognition system.
It keeps intuitions, organizes research, restores project context, and gradually
accumulates real signals for cross-domain analogy, falsifiable hypotheses and
experiment loops.

## Cognitive entrance

Experience becomes a typed **Input Episode**, then a non-factual **Reflection**
that records why it matters, what changed, structural connections, conflicts
and questions. Daily Dream digests; Weekly Dream produces labeled Cognitive
Synthesis. These layers help a human reinterpret evidence without becoming
Evidence themselves, and they do not execute tasks or experiments.

## Four graphs (long-term)

| Graph | Holds | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence** | Source, Work, Claim, Evidence, Support, Contradiction, Applicability, Temporal Validity | What evidence did the world provide? |
| **Model** | Phenomenon, Mechanism, Assumption, Constraint, Objective, Invariant, Failure Mode, Method, Prediction | How do we currently understand the world? |
| **Possibility** | Intuition, Anomaly, Tension, Analogy, Counterfactual, Hypothesis, Open Question | What might be true? |
| **Experiment** | Hypothesis, Competing Hypothesis, Prediction, Falsifier, Experiment, Metric, Result, Failure, Unexpected Observation, Decision | How would we know? |

## Signals before automation

Today the system records the usage signals those graphs will need later: why
someone saved material, what surprised them, what it might relate to, which
objects were used in projects, and whether a connection felt obvious, forced,
interesting or actionable.

It does **not** auto-generate analogies, hypotheses or experiments, and it does
not change Trust, Epistemic Status or Canonical from those signals alone.

Order of operations: record real research use first; design automatic discovery
capabilities only from that evidence.
