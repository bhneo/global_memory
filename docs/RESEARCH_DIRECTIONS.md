# Research Direction Registry

This registry defines the user-approved organization used by recurring
cognitive synthesis. It is a research-navigation contract, not Evidence, a
Memory Tier, or a scientific truth layer. Weekly cadence discovers candidates;
direction and mechanism determine synthesis scope.

## Routing contract

Each selected Reflection receives exactly one `primary_direction`, zero to two
`secondary_directions`, one or more `subdirections`, optional
`crosscut_dimensions`, a routing confidence, and a specific reason.

- The primary direction identifies the main research question changed by the
  source, not merely a method named in the title.
- A secondary direction is allowed only when the source contributes a distinct
  mechanism or boundary to that direction.
- Crosscuts compare recurring interfaces inside or across directions. They are
  not substitutes for a primary direction.
- Low-confidence routing retains Global fallback and must not force a new
  Synthesis.
- The same Reflection may support more than one scoped Synthesis only when each
  use declares a different role or delta. Silent duplicate aggregation is
  forbidden.

## Cross-direction discovery

Cross-direction output is optional and remains
`truth_layer: cognitive_synthesis`, without Memory Tier and with
`execution_safe: false`. Every candidate must identify at least two directions,
supporting Reflections and Sources, `shared_mechanism`, `boundary`,
`difference`, research value, counterarguments, evidence gaps, a verification
path, and confidence. Shared terminology, co-occurrence, or calendar proximity
alone is invalid.

Direction Synthesis should precede cross-direction Synthesis. A cross-direction
artifact cites the active direction Syntheses it compares, while the supporting
Reflection and Source chain remains authoritative for verification.
